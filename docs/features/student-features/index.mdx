# Student Features

Open TutorAI gives students a personal learning space where they can follow
teacher-led courses, create their own tutoring sessions, complete assignments,
and learn with an AI tutor. This page covers the features available to a student
account.

:::tip Getting started
1. Create an account and sign in. *(The first account on a new instance becomes the administrator.)*
2. Join a classroom with a course code, or create your own **Support** (personalized tutoring session).
3. Start your session and learn with the **avatar tutor**.
:::

---

## 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard is the student's home screen. It gives a single view of ongoing
work and learning activity, including the status of courses, supports, and
assignments (such as *pending*, *in-progress*, and *completed* items), so
students can quickly pick up where they left off.

## 🏫 Classrooms & Courses

Students access the classrooms and courses they are enrolled in from one place.
Enrolment is done with a **course code** provided by a teacher. From a classroom
a student can open course materials, related supports, and assignments.

## 🎓 Supports (personalized tutoring)

**Supports** are personalized tutoring sessions a student creates for extra
practice or self-study. A step-by-step wizard guides the setup so the AI tutor
can tailor the session:

1. **Subject** — pick a subject or enter a custom one.
2. **Course** — choose the relevant course or topic.
3. **Objectives** — describe what you want to achieve.
4. **Level** — set the difficulty to match your current understanding.
5. **Details** — add any extra context for the session.
6. **Review** — confirm your choices, then start the tutoring chat.

Once created, a support can be opened, edited, or revisited at any time from the
student's list of supports.

:::info You control your learning path
Supports let students decide how they learn — whether revising existing material,
preparing for an exam, or building a new skill from scratch — independently of
teacher-led courses.
:::

## 📝 Assignments

:::warning In development
This feature is still under development and may not be available in the current
release.
:::

The assignments section lists the coursework a student needs to complete. Each
assignment shows its **status** (for example *pending*, *in-progress*,
*completed*, or *overdue*) and its points, and students can **submit** their work
directly from this view.

## 💬 Messages

:::warning In development
This feature is still under development and may not be available in the current
release.
:::

The messaging system connects students with their teachers and classmates:

- **Group chats** — course-specific discussion groups.
- **Direct messages** — one-to-one conversations with teachers or classmates.

## 🧑‍🏫 Avatar & Voice/Video Mode

Open TutorAI CE includes an **avatar-powered discussion mode** with voice and
video, giving students an interactive, face-to-face style of tutoring.

## ⚙️ Settings

The settings area lets students manage their account and preferences, including
profile details, interface language (Arabic, French, English), theme, and
notifications.
