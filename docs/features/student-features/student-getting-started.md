# 👩‍🎓 Getting Started as a Student 

Welcome to OpenTutorAI! This guide will help you get started 
with the platform step by step.

🌐 **Access the platform at:** http://91.134.90.205:5173/

![OpenTutorAI Home Page](/img/student-guide/home.png)

---

## Step 1 — Sign Up and Create your Account

Click on **Sign Up** to create a new account.

![Sign in page](/img/student-guide/Login.gif)

Choose your role: **Student**

![Choose your role](/img/student-guide/role.gif)

:::info Fill in your information
- Enter your **First name** and **Last name**
- Enter a valid **Email address**
- Choose a **Password** you can remember
- ✅ Don't forget to check **"I agree to the Terms of Service"**
- Then click **Create Account**
:::

![Create account](/img/student-guide/signup.gif)

---

## Step 2 — Access your Student Dashboard

After logging in, you arrive on your **Dashboard**.

![Dashboard](/img/student-guide/dashboard.png)

:::tip What you can see on your Dashboard
- Your **supports** (learning sessions)
- Your **progress**
- Your **assignments**
:::

---

## Step 3 — Create a Learning Support

1. Click on ➕ **Support**

![Click Support](/img/student-guide/support-btn.gif)
   Then Click On **Create My Support**
![Click On Create My Support](/img/student-guide/support-btn_Create.gif)

2. Fill in the **title** and a short **description** of your goal

![support form](/img/student-guide/support-form.png)

:::info Example
- **Title:** Learn Python Basics for Beginners  
- **Description:** I want to learn the fundamentals of Python programming, including variables, loops, functions, and basic problem-solving. My goal is to build simple programs and understand core programming concepts.
- Choose your **subject** 
- Click **Continue**
:::
![support form Example](/img/student-guide/support-form_Example.gif)

3. Upload a file if available **(PDF / Word / PPT)** — or skip

![Upload a file](/img/student-guide/Upload_a_file.gif)

4. Define your **learning objective**

![learning objective](/img/student-guide/learning_objective.jpg)

5. Select your **level** (Primary / Middle / High School / University)

![Upload a file](/img/student-guide/level.jpg)

6. Choose your **language** and **duration**

![language_duration](/img/student-guide/language_duration.png)

7. Click **Continue**


---

## Step 4 — Start Learning

1. Review all your information : Title, Objective, Level
2. Click **Start Learning**

![Review support](/img/student-guide/review.gif)

3. Choose your chat experience :

![Choose Your Modele](/img/student-guide/chat-Modele.gif)
![Choose experience](/img/student-guide/chat-choice.gif)

4. Choose your chat modes :
:::tip Two modes available
- 💬 **Text Chat** — Fast, efficient, supports code blocks
- 🤖 **Avatar Chat** — 3D avatar with voice and animations
:::
![chat modes](/img/student-guide/Chat_modes.gif)
---

## 🎉 You're all set!

You can now start learning with your AI tutor.
Ask questions, upload documents

![start learning](/img/student-guide/start_learning.png)

and track your progress directly from your dashboard.

![progress](/img/student-guide/progress.png)
